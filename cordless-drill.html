DIY buying guide

Choosing a cordless drill for home projects

A drill-driver is useful for furniture assembly, pilot holes, and basic home repairs. Focus on the work you actually plan to do.

Quick answer: For occasional household work, prioritize a comfortable drill-driver kit with battery and charger over the highest advertised power.

Drill-driver or hammer drill

A standard drill-driver handles common wood and metal tasks with appropriate bits. Masonry may require a hammer function and suitable masonry bits.

Battery ecosystem

Consider whether the brand’s battery works with other tools you may buy later, and check what batteries and chargers are included.

Weight and grip

Compact tools can be easier in cabinets and overhead. Compare overall weight with a battery installed.

Chuck and controls

Check chuck capacity, clutch settings, variable speed, and reverse direction. A clutch helps reduce overdriving screws.
